Is it right for you?

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What are permanent outdoor lights?

They are LED lights you mount once along your roofline or eaves and leave up all year. Instead of hanging new string lights for each holiday, you change the colors and effects from an app. The Govee Permanent Outdoor Lights 2 is one example.

Can I install them myself?

Many homeowners install them on their own. The lights mount with VHB adhesive and clips, and you will need a ladder for the one-time setup. Plan for it to take a while. Always follow Govee's official instructions, and hire a professional if you are not comfortable working on a ladder.

Can they stay up in rain, snow and summer heat?

Govee lists an IP67 waterproof rating and an operating range of -4°F to 140°F. Check the official product page (opens in a new tab) to confirm the details for your climate.

Which length should I buy?

Measure the rooflines you want to light, then compare that with the available lengths: 50 ft, 100 ft and 150 ft. The official product page (opens in a new tab) shows current options.

Do I need Wi-Fi?

Smart features like app, voice and Matter control rely on your home network and the Govee app. Check the official requirements before you buy, especially if your Wi-Fi is weak near the front of the house.

Does it work with Alexa, Google Assistant or Matter?

Govee lists support for Alexa, Google Assistant and Matter. Smart home setups vary, so confirm compatibility with your devices on the official product page (opens in a new tab).
